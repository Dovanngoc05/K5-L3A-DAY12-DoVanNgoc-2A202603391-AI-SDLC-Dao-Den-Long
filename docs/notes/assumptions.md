# Documentation Notes

## Phase 1 Assumptions & Tuning Parameters
- Night 1 Duration: 300 seconds (18,000 ticks at 60Hz).
- Lanterns count: 5 lanterns (L1, L2, L3, L4, L5) located in circle radius 25m.
- Lantern cost: 3 motes per lantern.
- Player: speed 6.0 m/s, carry capacity 5 motes, initial flames = 3.
- Pulse: cooldown 3.0s, push distance 3.0m, stun duration 2.0s, cost 1 flame/light.
- Shades: initial speed 3.5 m/s, hit radius 1.0m.
- Invulnerability after hit: 1.5s.
- Hit drop ratio: 0.5 (half of carried motes dropped).
